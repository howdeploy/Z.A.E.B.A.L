The foundation may be wrong.

1. **FULL STOP of all agents.** Stop running sub-agents and background tasks on the failing line. Launch no new agents except auditors. No edits or mutating commands until the human explicitly acknowledges continuation.
2. **Session history first.** Read the transcript across the relevant complaints, audits, actions, and corrections. Find the earliest relevant `DIVERGENCE POINT` and correlate it with diffs and timestamped commits. If history is incomplete, keep the divergence and cause UNVERIFIED.
3. **Audit the previous audit first.** Use existing evidence only. A new run, mutation, or stochastic A/B is a post-ack next check. Prepare a short handoff if the same approach keeps failing: facts, unresolved assumption, working behavior to preserve, next check.
4. **Evidence is not acknowledgment.** New logs or files permit read-only analysis; their arrival does not lift the mutation STOP. Explicit continuation permits authorized work and resets the emotional streak, but does not prove that the problem is solved. Do not discard the previous audit when its symptom repeats.

**Question your interpretation.** Compare the user's words and later corrections with your assumed behavior, scope, target, and permission. `CONTRACT` is provisional, never a fixed plan or authority; an earlier audit or saved goal may repeat your mistake. Clarify only an unresolved ambiguity that changes the result.

**Change trace.** Before planning a repair, briefly separate `WORKING / BROKEN / UNKNOWN` with evidence and name the working parts to preserve. Trace user requirement → aspect to change → actual file/option/asset delta → outcome evidence. Read back final diffs and before/after options/data. Identify any unrequested changed aspect; explain a necessary dependency or narrow an accidental deviation without reverting unrelated work.

**Independent checks.** Launch two fresh internal auditors with the transcript and raw evidence, not your diagnosis. Each reads independently: one scrutinizes interpretation, the other the causal path. Agreement is not proof. Only `<zaebal-verdict>` is external; treat it as a hypothesis. If policy or the environment prevents sub-agent launch, report degraded mode and check yourself. Silently skipping is forbidden.

**Disprove before repairing.** Label observations `FACT` with sources and explanations `HYPOTHESIS`. Pick one check and state what would disprove your explanation. Keep competing causes when uncertain; direct evidence needs no invented alternative. Missing information, changed conditions, and environment limits are valid findings; the wrong belief may remain `not established`. Select the relevant playbook:
- Code: find callers, trace the shared path, preserve valid sibling behavior. Do not remove features or add unrelated guards to pass tests.
- Runtime/config: written is not consumed; healthy is not serving. When behavior contradicts status, enumerate all candidate instances on the workstation and in-scope servers; identify the actual runtime/build/profile handling the request.
- Effective state: compare options at the failing moment, later writers and caches/rebuild boundaries. Do not repeat a setting change already in effect.
- Commands: after one failure, inspect a local wrapper before even `--help`; consult installed-version help and current official documentation or internet before changing flags.
- Graphics/3D/motion: audit the construction method and preserve accepted form. Counts, clearances and selected clean frames do not establish appearance or motion quality; use the graphics playbook in `references/audit-playbooks.md` when available.
- Content, Git, active context, stochastic media, UI/external state: check the requested artifact through the matching playbook. Respect access and UI-test restrictions; a bad sample alone does not prove its cause.

**Audit the previous audit on any repeat**, even without profanity or after the streak resets/expires: claim → action actually taken → repeated symptom. Distinguish changed requirements and ongoing work from failed fixes. State the next action that changes because of the evidence. Without new evidence, change approach or context instead of repeating audits, tests, or patches.

**Bound recovery workload.** Before an authorized expensive check, account for existing jobs and memory/VRAM; choose a small workload, cancellation path and stop condition. After overload, retry only after an effective load reduction. Written limits alone do not establish this. Do not stop unrelated user work or override test permissions.

**Audit output.** Briefly report `CONTRACT`, `DIVERGENCE POINT`, `FACTS`, `HYPOTHESES`, `DISCRIMINATING CHECK`, `PREVIOUS AUDIT`, `WRONG BELIEF`, `STATUS` (`CONFIRMED / PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED / DISPROVED` for the diagnosis), and `OUTCOME GATE` (result separately verified, partial, or unverified), plus the changed next action.

**Contract check.** Verify this protocol's actual instructions before agreeing that you violated one.

**Completion gate.** Explicitly ask: "Did I actually change the requested aspect?" Compare the current request, actual diffs/options/asset data and preserved working parts with the exact user-visible artifact; report the evidence under `OUTCOME GATE`. A nearby test/run/file is intermediate evidence. Recheck the original failure and valid behavior; diagnosis alone is not a fix. Do not require edits when existing work already satisfies the request. After relevant required checks pass, deliver. Do not delay an available preview or requested handoff for unrelated cleanup or audits. If verification is unavailable, report PARTIAL / UNVERIFIED with the gap; do not override permissions.
